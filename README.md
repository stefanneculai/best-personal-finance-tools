## What changed in 2026

The personal finance tool landscape shifted in 2024–2026. A few changes worth knowing before you pick anything from the list below:

* **Mint shut down** in March 2024 and was folded into Credit Karma, which most former Mint users describe as a downgrade. If you are still looking for a replacement, see the curated [Mint alternatives in 2026](https://www.financialaha.com/alternatives/mint/) and the broader [Mint replacement comparison](https://www.financialaha.com/alternatives/mint-replacement/).
* **Personal Capital rebranded to Empower** in 2023. Old reviews still call it Personal Capital. Same product, new name. Comparison: [Empower alternatives](https://www.financialaha.com/alternatives/empower/).
* **Subscription fatigue is real.** Most budgeting apps moved to recurring pricing in 2025. The spreadsheet template movement (one-time pay, own your data forever) saw a renaissance as a result.
* **Bank-connected apps face stricter privacy scrutiny** under updated US state laws and the EU's PSD3 rollout. Several once-popular tools either shut down or limited features in 2025.
* **AI assistants in personal finance** matured. Cleo, Copilot Money, and similar tools added LLM-powered features. Worth a look if you like conversational interfaces.
